# 🎓 Chinese Characters Learning Platform

An interactive Chinese character learning application combining study, pronunciation, stroke order, and writing practice.

## ✨ Features

- **📚 Learn Common Chinese Characters** - Contains 200+ frequently used characters, organized by category
- **🔊 Native Pronunciation** - Click to hear standard Mandarin Chinese pronunciation
- **🎯 Pinyin Annotations** - Complete pinyin and stroke count information
- **✍️ Stroke Order Practice** - Review each character's stroke count sequence before writing on canvas
- **💾 Undo Function** - Undo up to 10 recent strokes
- **🔍 Search and Filter** - Filter characters by category and keywords
- **📱 Responsive Design** - Perfect display on desktop and mobile devices

## 🎓 Character Categories

- **Basic** - Common pronouns and conjunctions (I, you, he, she, is, have, and, etc.)
- **Verbs** - Action verbs (do, come, go, say, read, write, draw, sing, etc.)
- **Animals** - Animal-related characters (cat, dog, fish, bird, elephant, etc.)
- **Nature** - Natural elements (mountain, water, fire, wood, earth, sky, etc.)
- **Numbers** - Numerals and counting words (one, two, three, etc.)
- **Food** - Food and objects (rice, meal, vegetables, meat, fruit, etc.)
- **Clothing** - Clothing and body parts (clothes, pants, shoes, hat, head, etc.)
- **Family** - Family and relationships (father, mother, brother, sister, etc.)
- **School** - Education-related words (school, class, book, pen, paper, etc.)
- **Colors** - Color names (red, green, blue, yellow, white, black, etc.)
- **Seasons/Time** - Seasons and time expressions (spring, summer, autumn, winter, etc.)
- **Places** - Places and buildings (home, house, door, window, wall, etc.)
- **Transportation** - Vehicles and transportation (bicycle, airplane, train, car, etc.)
- **Professions** - Profession and job-related words (teacher, doctor, police, etc.)

## 🎮 User Guide

### Learning Mode

1. **Select a Character**
   - Click any character in the left grid
   - Or use the search box to find a specific character
   - Use category buttons to filter characters

2. **View Character Information**
   - Large character display in the center
   - Pinyin, stroke count, meaning, and example below
   - Click 🔊 Pronunciation button to hear standard pronunciation

3. **Stroke Order Practice**
   - Left side shows the character, total strokes, and numbered stroke order steps
   - Right side canvas for writing practice
   - Customize brush color and size

### Writing Controls

- **Brush Color** - Choose your preferred brush color
- **Brush Size** - Adjust brush thickness (1-40 pixels)
- **↶ Undo** - Undo recent strokes (up to 10 steps)
- **🗑️ Clear** - Clear the entire canvas
- **👁️ Hint** - Show a semi-transparent character guide for reference

### Navigation

- **← Previous** - View previous character
- **Next →** - View next character
- **Counter** - Shows current position and total count

## 💡 Learning Tips

1. **Daily Practice** - Learn 3-5 new characters per day
2. **Repeated Writing** - Write each character at least 10 times
3. **Listen to Pronunciation** - Listen to standard pronunciation multiple times
4. **Respect Stroke Order** - Follow the correct Chinese character stroke order
5. **Practical Application** - Use learned characters in daily life
